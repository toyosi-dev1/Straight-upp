/about

Who I am

Designing visuals for ideas that don't exist yet.

I'm a Graduate of Computer Science Education at Lagos State University, building a practice at the intersection of AI-assisted creativity and front-end development. I use AI as part of a larger creative process — concept development, art direction, and visual storytelling — not as a shortcut around it.

On the technical side, I work in React and JavaScript, with AI-assisted backend logic where it's useful. My prompt engineering practice covers lighting setups, lens language, material behavior, and consistency techniques like seed locking and reference-image anchoring — the same discipline I bring to design systems and digital experiences.

/process

How the work gets made

  1. 01

    Explore

    Research, references, ideas, and creative possibilities before a single prompt is written.

  2. 02

    Direct

    Develop the concept, visual language, mood, and art direction — the brief a prompt has to serve.

  3. 03

    Generate

    Use AI and creative tools to explore and develop unique visual outcomes against that direction.

  4. 04

    Refine

    Curate, edit, and transform raw generations into polished, IP-clean creative work.

  5. 05

    Build

    Bring selected concepts to life through thoughtful front-end design and digital experiences.
